How the numbers are made

Every day the open job postings are downloaded from the Bundesagentur für Arbeit. Contact details are removed, then an AI model reads each posting and returns its role, seniority, home office, required languages and technical skills, each marked as required or nice to have. A share is the number of postings that ask for something, divided by all postings in the chosen role and region.

Product names that a posting does not actually mention are left out, because the model most likely added them. Jobs that are not tech work are not counted. A posting with places in two states counts in both.
